## Evidence rules

- **After** means the public source visibly shows a verified completed result.
- **Current** means the screenshot records the source as it exists now; it is not automatically a completed fix.
- A sent message, approval, prepared change, or uploaded file is not labeled **After** unless the public page was independently verified.
- Google receives its own after capture only when a clean public Search or Images check proves a refresh.
