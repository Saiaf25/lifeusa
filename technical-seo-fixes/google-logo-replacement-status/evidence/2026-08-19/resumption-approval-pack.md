### Option A: Make the obsolete archive private

**Recommendation:** Preferred if the archive has no continuing operational purpose.

### Option B: Keep it public and fully rebrand it

Use only when leadership cannot yet decide between A and B. Record an owner, recovery route, and decision deadline. This does not solve the public retired-logo source and cannot count as completion.
