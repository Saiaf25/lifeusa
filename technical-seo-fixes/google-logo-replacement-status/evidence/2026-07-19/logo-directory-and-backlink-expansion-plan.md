# LifeUSA logo directory and backlink expansion plan

Prepared July 19, 2026. This document adds a controlled new-placement track to the Google logo replacement program. It is a research and approval artifact only. No profile was claimed, created, submitted, edited, or paid for.

## Purpose

Publish the approved LifeUSA logo and canonical website link on a small set of authoritative, relevant profiles that can strengthen public entity consistency and create useful discovery paths. New listings support the campaign, but they do not force Google to select a particular logo and do not replace source-level removal of the retired crescent mark.

## Logo classification used by this plan

- **Canonical current logo:** the yellow-ray square and horizontal Wix assets. Use the canonical square for every new directory or business-profile placement.
- **Acceptable current variant:** the blue-globe logo used on Facebook and matching official profiles. It is current and acceptable, not outdated or retired. Existing profiles do not require a logo change merely because they use this variant.
- **Retired logo:** the crescent mark still exposed by the old WordPress archive, Kids That Do Good, and Arab Info Mall. This remains the removal and correction target.

The canonical asset is preferred for new placements to make the new signal consistent. That preference does not make the approved blue-globe variant defective.

## Qualification rule

A new placement is eligible only when the platform:

1. genuinely fits LifeUSA as a US nonprofit, humanitarian organization, employer, volunteer organization, or Southfield-based entity;
2. permits an authorized representative to claim or create the profile;
3. supports a public logo or profile image and a public website field, or is an unusually strong identity source worth testing even when logo support is not guaranteed;
4. publishes a stable, publicly accessible profile that can be verified logged out;
5. does not sell bulk links, require keyword-stuffed copy, or operate primarily as a low-quality SEO directory.

No claim is made about `dofollow` status. The acceptance test is a visible, accurate entity profile and a working link to `https://www.lifeusa.org/`.

## Recommended order

1. **Existing authoritative profiles:** Google Business Profile, Candid, GreatNonprofits, Charity Navigator, and ReliefWeb.
2. **New nonprofit and giving profiles:** Every.org, PayPal Giving Fund, Benevity, Idealist, and VolunteerMatch.
3. **Local and business identity:** Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, and an existing Yelp page.
4. **Conditional or paid:** GlobalGiving, D&B, membership directories, and any candidate requiring a fee or formal program application.

## Per-placement approval and acceptance record

Every new claim, application, or account change remains human-gated. Before execution, create a scoped approval item naming the exact `DIR` ID, platform, account, cost, submitted fields, and external effect.

After an approved placement is published, record:

- approver and approval date;
- account owner and access route;
- submission and publication dates;
- public profile URL and direct image URL where available;
- visible logo classification;
- website-link destination and HTTP result;
- screenshot captured logged out;
- cost and renewal terms, if any;
- Google discovery date, if later observed.

Do not mark a placement complete merely because a form was submitted. Completion requires a live public profile showing the approved canonical logo, accurate LifeUSA identity, and a working canonical website link.
