# LifeUSA Google logo baseline

Captured July 15, 2026 at 1280 px desktop width.

## Protocol

- Query: `LifeUSA`
- Google locale parameters: English (`hl=en`), United States (`gl=us`)
- Personalization parameter: disabled (`pws=0`)
- Google Search and Google Images were captured in the same browser session.
- Google reported that the Search results were not personalized.
- Account identity and personal controls were excluded from the published screenshots.

## Baseline observation

Google Search correctly surfaces Life for Relief and Development's website and branded paid result, but the broad `LifeUSA` query also returns a similarly named language-learning company. Google Images mixes current LifeUSA website and social imagery with LifeUSA International, marketplace products, and other unrelated entities. This confirms that the campaign must strengthen consistent owned and authoritative logo signals instead of relying on generic directory volume.

The first-viewport capture is complete. The structured first-20 image-source inventory remains an active action because the full ranking set can change by time, location, and Google interface behavior.
