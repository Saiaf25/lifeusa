Problem 1: Subdomain Sprawl

Five sites pretending to be one

Google treats every subdomain as a separate website. Authority earned on one does not lift the others, so your equity is split five ways.

staff.lifeusa.org: a public WordPress site titled "My WordPress Blog".

donation.lifeusa.org: a second donation platform (Givecloud).

donate.lifeusa.org: the primary donor portal (214 ref domains).

Two donation platforms means your donor data is split and you cannot see your true conversion picture. A public staff WordPress site exposes internal content and competes with your brand. arabic.lifeusa.org is dead in DNS, its backlink is wasted entirely.

Problem 2: Language Signals

The language-header bug: then vs now

What we found in May

URL: / content-language: zh-CN URL: /about content-language: tr-TR URL: /gaza content-language: fr-FR URL: /ar content-language: en

Wix was telling Google your English pages were Chinese, Turkish and French, a strong explanation for why content did not surface in English search.

What it returns now (5 June)

URL: / content-language: en-GB URL: /about content-language: en-US URL: /gaza content-language: en-US URL: /ar content-language: en-US

The worst of the bug has cleared, pages now report English. But two issues remain: the locale flips between en-GB / en-US inconsistently, and your /ar Arabic page still reports English, not Arabic.

Why this still matters: the /ar page declaring itself "en-US" tells Google your Arabic content is English. That directly feeds the original complaint, Arabic articles not showing up correctly. We should still open a Wix ticket to pin the header per language. صفحتك العربية ما زالت تخبر جوجل أنها إنجليزية, وهذا يفسر عدم ظهور مقالاتك العربية بشكل صحيح.
Problem 2: Continued

Your Arabic is tagged "Jordanian only"

Your hreflang tags exist, good. But the Arabic version is declared as ar-jo (Jordanian Arabic). For a global humanitarian charity serving Arabic speakers worldwide, this narrows the audience Google will show your content to.

The fix is one line in Wix Multilingual settings: change ar-jo → ar. This widens your Arabic reach from one country to the entire Arabic-speaking world. إصلاح من سطر واحد: تغيير ar-jo إلى ar يوسع وصولك لكل العالم العربي.

Current homepage tags

<link rel="alternate" href="…/" hreflang="x-default"/> <link rel="alternate" href="…/ar" hreflang="ar-jo"/> <link rel="alternate" href="…/" hreflang="en-us"/>

www.lifeusa.org/ar: the Arabic experience exists and renders well.

Problem 3: Indexation

35% of your pages say "don't list me"

122 of 345 crawlable pages carry a noindex directive, an instruction telling Google to drop them from results.

On a site with only 33 actively ranking pages, a 35% noindex rate almost certainly contains pages that should be visible. Every accidental noindex is a page that can never rank, never drive a donation, and never earn credit for its backlinks, no matter how good the content is.

Action: with Search Console access, we walk the 122-URL list and separate intentional from accidental. This is often the fastest "found money" fix on a site.

Problem 4: Images Invisible to Search

Your images can't be read

451 images have an empty alt attribute and 20 have none at all, 471 in total. When you publish an Arabic article with photos, search engines have no text describing what the image shows, so it cannot be indexed for image search in any language.

This is the second half of your original concern: "my images don't appear in search." This is exactly why.

Action: alt text on the top 50 traffic pages first, then a site-wide content workflow so every new upload gets described. نص بديل وصفي لكل صورة, يبدأ بأهم 50 صفحة ثم يصبح عادة عند كل رفع.

Problem 5: Page Experience

The page jumps, and it's slow

1,035 images (90%) have no width or height defined. The browser doesn't know how much space to reserve, so the layout jumps as the page loads, measured by Google as Cumulative Layout Shift, a ranking signal.

543 images (47%) are over 100 KB, slowing down how fast the main content appears (Largest Contentful Paint). Both pull rankings down on every page and push donors on slow phones to leave before the Donate button loads.

Action: add dimensions site-wide (Wix can do this), compress images over 100 KB, and serve modern formats.

Problem 6: Structured Data

Google thinks you're a for-profit business

Your homepage declares the organization as ProfessionalService, a for-profit business type in Google's vocabulary. The correct type for a registered nonprofit is NGO.

The wrong type blocks nonprofit-specific Google features: the donations panel, charity disambiguation, and trust badges. And your 463 blog posts carry no Article schema, the single largest rich-result opportunity on the site (author, date, headline image in search).

Action: switch homepage to NGO + add DonateAction; enable Article schema in the Wix Blog SEO panel across all posts.

Your Original Question

"Why don't my Arabic articles and images show up?"

Three causes stacked on top of each other. Each one alone would hurt; together they explain the complaint completely.

Cause 1

Language confusion

The Arabic /ar page reports itself to Google as English (en-US). Google can't place it as Arabic content.

Cause 2

Narrow locale

hreflang says ar-jo, Jordanian Arabic only, shrinking the audience Google shows it to.

Cause 3

Unreadable images

471 images have no descriptive text, so they can't be indexed for image search in any language.

The good news: all three are fixable without a rebuild, one Wix ticket, one settings change, one content workflow. This is the highest-leverage cluster on the whole property. الأسباب الثلاثة قابلة للإصلاح بدون إعادة بناء, تذكرة دعم، إعداد واحد، وروتين محتوى.
