LESSON 9 · Ahrefs

Read keyword metrics without choosing on one number

Ahrefs metrics help screen a keyword. They do not decide whether LifeUSA should create or change a page.

10–15 minutesSEO beginnerOne metric record
AhrefsKeyword and competitor estimates
By the end: you can explain Volume, Traffic Potential, and KD, then decide whether one relevant phrase deserves a SERP review.

Three estimates, three different questions

Volume

Estimated average monthly searches for the exact keyword in the selected country.

Traffic Potential

Estimated organic traffic to the current number-one page for the Parent topic, across its ranking keywords.

Keyword Difficulty

Ahrefs’ 0–100 backlink-based estimate of first-page difficulty. It excludes on-page factors.

No single metric approves a keyword. First confirm LifeUSA relevance; then inspect the current SERP to understand the search need.

NOW USE A LIFEUSA WORKFLOW

Record one keyword without approving it

Use live Ahrefs only with approved access. Otherwise, use the screen share or artifact Saiaf provides.

  1. Open Keywords Explorer and select United States.
  2. Enter one approved LifeUSA phrase. For practice, use orphan sponsorship.
  3. Record the keyword, country, and date checked.
  4. Copy Volume, KD, Traffic Potential, and Parent topic exactly. Write “Not shown” for a missing field.
  5. Write one sentence explaining whether the phrase answers a genuine LifeUSA donor question.
  6. Choose only one next step: Inspect the SERP, Park the keyword, or Ask Saiaf. Do not approve an article.
