Refine one SEO brief with a precise follow-up

A useful follow-up says what to preserve, what to change, how to check it, and which actions remain prohibited.

10–15 minutesSEO beginnerOne review-ready brief
Codexby OpenAI · source-grounded review
By the end: you can choose one focused follow-up that fixes the reviewed draft while preserving correct evidence and scope.

Revise without losing the evidence

Preserve

Exact source facts and any wording already verified.

Change

The unsupported, unclear, or overconfident part identified in the claim review.

Check

A final test that every figure and factual statement still matches the source.

Boundary

No browsing, editing, sending, or publishing; the result remains a review draft.

A follow-up can improve the existing draft. You do not need to start over or expand the task.

COPYABLE EXAMPLE · PRACTICE OR ADAPT

Focused revision request

Revise the LifeUSA page opportunity brief.

PRESERVE
The exact page URL, query, impressions, CTR, and position from the source card.

CHANGE
Remove “people ignore this page” because the source does not prove reader feelings.
Present “test a clearer title” as a recommendation, not a guaranteed result.

FORMAT
Keep the brief under 120 words. Use Evidence, Interpretation, Test, and Missing information.

FINAL CHECK
Confirm every figure matches the source. Flag anything you could not verify.

BOUNDARY
Do not browse, edit the website, send, or publish anything.
  1. Choose the most important unsupported or unclear part.
  2. Write one follow-up naming what to preserve, what to change, the required format, and the boundary.
  3. Ask Codex to revise the existing draft rather than start over.
  4. Compare the before-and-after versions. Confirm every figure still matches the source and every recommendation is labeled.
  5. Save the review-ready brief. Do not send, publish, or change the live page.
Review-ready does not mean approved. Bring the final brief and its source card to the Wednesday review before any content decision or live action.

PASS CHECK · RETRIEVE, DON’T REREAD

Recall the four-part follow-up

“My follow-up says what to preserve, what to change, what to check, and what remains prohibited. The final brief is ready for ___, not ___.”

  • I preserved every verified figure exactly.
  • I removed or qualified unsupported certainty.
  • I kept one reviewable test.
  • I saved a draft for review and took no live action.

Finish by bringing the source, claim review, and final brief to the Wednesday meeting. Approval and publication remain separate decisions.
