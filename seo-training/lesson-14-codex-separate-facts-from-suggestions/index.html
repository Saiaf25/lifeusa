LESSON 14 · Codex

Separate facts from suggestions

A source can report what happened. Interpretation and suggestions may be useful, but they must not be presented as confirmed facts.

10–15 minutesSEO beginnerOne source map
Codexby OpenAI · source-grounded review
By the end: you can decide whether each sentence is a confirmed fact, interpretation, suggestion, or missing information.

Four labels prevent accidental certainty

Confirmed fact

Directly supported by a named field or statement in the supplied source.

Interpretation

A cautious explanation consistent with the evidence but not proven by it.

Suggestion

A change or test proposed for review, not a guaranteed result.

Missing information

A question the current source cannot answer.

Check every “confirmed fact” yourself. Codex can classify text, but the human reviewer must compare each fact with the original source.
