LESSON 11 · Ahrefs

Find one competitor page to study

Site Explorer can reveal pages worth studying. Its traffic figures are estimates, not a competitor’s private analytics.

10–15 minutesSEO beginnerOne page observation
By the end: you can select one relevant comparison page from Top Pages and describe a useful pattern without treating estimates as analytics or content as a blueprint.

Study the page, respect the boundary

Useful observation

A donor question, page format, or information pattern that may deserve comparison.

Unsafe shortcut

Copying wording, claims, brand terms, structure, or program details from another organization.

Relevance beats the biggest traffic estimate. Choose the page closest to the LifeUSA question you are researching.
Official source:
  • Ahrefs: Site Explorer traffic and Top Pages

PRACTICE · GET IMMEDIATE FEEDBACK

Top Pages shows 12,000 monthly visits for a comparison page. What can Angela safely report?

Practice boundary: Ahrefs models another site’s organic traffic; it does not open that site’s private analytics.

Choose an answer to see feedback.

NOW USE A LIFEUSA WORKFLOW

Choose one relevant Top Pages row

Use only a comparison domain approved for this exercise. If no domain is supplied, complete the quiz and stop.

  1. Open Site Explorer and enter the approved comparison domain.
  2. Select Domain mode and the United States report.
  3. Open Organic search → Top pages. Narrow the report to the approved topic, such as URLs containing orphan.
  4. Review the first five relevant rows. Choose the page that answers the closest donor question, not merely the row with the largest estimate.
  5. Record the URL, title, Ahrefs-estimated organic traffic, and top keyword if shown.
  6. Write one pattern worth studying and one thing LifeUSA must not copy. Do not treat the other organization’s brand name as a LifeUSA keyword.
Do not create a new export without approval. If access is limited, use Saiaf’s small approved CSV or screen share and keep the target, mode, country, and date attached.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Complete one comparison-page observation

PASS CHECK · RETRIEVE, DON’T REREAD

State the estimate boundary

“Ahrefs’ comparison-site traffic number is an ___, not the organization’s private ___. I chose this page because ___.”

  • I used an approved comparison domain and country.
  • I chose relevance instead of the biggest estimate.
  • I called the traffic figure an estimate.
  • I recorded a learning pattern and a no-copy boundary.

If the comparison domain or report scope is unclear, ask Saiaf before searching. A wrong domain or mode changes the evidence.
