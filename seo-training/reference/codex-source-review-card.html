Prompt, ground, review, and refine

Use after Lessons 13–16. Codex can organize SEO evidence; its output does not replace the source or the human review.

The four-step check

  1. Prompt: name the Goal, Context, Output, and Boundaries.
  2. Ground: separate confirmed facts, interpretations, suggestions, and missing information.
  3. Review: check every factual claim against the original source yourself.
  4. Refine: preserve evidence, fix one reviewed issue, run a final check, and save a draft.

Boundaries to remember

  • Use only the approved source.
  • Flag missing information instead of guessing.
  • Do not browse, edit, send, or publish.
  • Review-ready is not approved or live.
When uncertain: preserve the exact source, label the unknown, and bring the question to Saiaf before any live action.
