Compare search performance fairly

Use this after Lesson 3 before describing a page as improving or declining.

The fair-comparison rule

Previous 28 daysSame page
VERSUS
Last 28 daysSame page
  1. Filter to one page.
  2. Choose Date → Compare → Last 28 days to previous period.
  3. Read clicks and impressions for both periods.
  4. Say whether each metric rose, fell, or stayed similar.
  5. Note campaign or seasonal context without claiming it caused the change.
Safe wording: the data shows what changed, not why it changed.

Your reporting pattern

“Clicks ___ from ___ to ___, while impressions ___ from ___ to ___. This shows ___. It does not yet prove ___.”
