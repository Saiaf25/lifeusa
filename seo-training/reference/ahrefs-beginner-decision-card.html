Estimate, inspect, and decide carefully

Use after Lessons 9–12. Ahrefs helps research a candidate; it does not approve a keyword, page, or publication.

The four-step check

  1. Estimate: read Volume, Traffic Potential, and KD as different clues.
  2. Inspect: read the current SERP before deciding what searchers likely need.
  3. Compare: treat Top Pages traffic as an estimate and study patterns without copying.
  4. Classify: check the existing LifeUSA owner before labeling a Content Gap row.

Boundaries to remember

  • Missing metrics stay “Not shown.”
  • Estimated traffic is not private analytics.
  • A ranking gap is not proof of missing content.
  • Every output remains a candidate until review.
When uncertain: preserve the exact source, label the unknown, and bring the question to Saiaf before any live action.
