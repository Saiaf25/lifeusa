Read indexed and live results correctly

Use this after Lesson 4 whenever you inspect one page in Search Console.

Keep the two views separate

Google Index

Google’s stored knowledge from its most recent indexing of the page.

Live Test

Whether Google can access and process the page available right now.

  1. Record the exact Google Index verdict.
  2. Record the last crawl date.
  3. Record the Google-selected canonical.
  4. Run a Live Test only when current-page access needs checking.
  5. Record its verdict separately and stop before requesting indexing.
No guarantees: “URL can be indexed” does not mean the current version is indexed, appears in results, or will rank.

Your explanation pattern

“The Google Index result tells me ___. The Live Test tells me ___. Neither result guarantees ___.”
