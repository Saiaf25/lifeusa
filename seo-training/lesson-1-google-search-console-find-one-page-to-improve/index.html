LESSON 1 · GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE

Find one page worth improving

You do not need to understand every Search Console report. Today you will use three numbers to make one useful content decision.

10–15 minutesBeginnerOne practical output
By the end: you can choose one page that already appears in Google but may deserve a better title, description, or answer.

First, learn only these three numbers

ImpressionsHow often Google showed the page.
CTRWhat percentage of those views became clicks.
PositionWhere the page appeared on average.

CTR = clicks ÷ impressions × 100

The pattern to remember: useful visibility + near page one + weak clicking = an opportunity worth inspecting.

Primary source: Google’s official guide explains how to identify top queries and low-CTR pages in the Search results Performance report.

NOW USE LIFEUSA DATA

Repeat the decision in Search Console

If your access is not working, finish the practice above and tell Saiaf where you were blocked. Do not use a different property.

  1. Open Google Search Console and select the exact lifeusa.org property.
  2. Open Performance → Search results. Set the date to Last 3 months.
  3. Turn on Clicks, Impressions, Average CTR, and Average position.
  4. Open the Pages tab. Look for a page with meaningful impressions, an average position roughly between 4 and 15, and CTR that looks weak beside pages in a similar position.
  5. Click that page, then open Queries. Choose one query that clearly matches what the page should answer.
  6. Record the evidence below. Do not edit the live page yet.

Need help finding the report? Use Google’s official Performance report introduction.
