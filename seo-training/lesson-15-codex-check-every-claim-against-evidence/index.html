LESSON 15 · Codex

Check every claim against its evidence

Confident wording can hide an unsupported claim. A claim check makes the source and status visible before revision.

10–15 minutesSEO beginnerOne claim review
Codexby OpenAI · source-grounded review
By the end: you can decide whether a Codex draft is safe to use by checking each factual claim against the original source.

Three claim statuses

Supported

The supplied source directly supports the factual claim.

Unsupported

The source contradicts the claim or does not establish it.

Needs more evidence

The claim may be testable, but the current source cannot confirm it.

Ask Codex for a check, then review the check yourself. The final readiness decision remains human.
