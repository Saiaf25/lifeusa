# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are LifeUSA decision makers, website administrators, and the SEO consultant managing the logo remediation program. They review the page during status updates to understand what is complete, what remains open, which evidence supports each claim, and which decisions require human approval.

## Product Purpose

The tracker provides one evidence-backed view of the LifeUSA Google logo replacement program. Success means a client can quickly understand the current state, distinguish completed work from pending verification, inspect proof, and identify the next decision without needing technical SEO knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. The interface should feel professional and humanitarian without appearing corporate, decorative, or promotional. Client-facing language must be direct, brief, and free of unnecessary jargon.

## Anti-references

- Dense technical dashboards that require specialist knowledge.
- Repeated nested cards with weak hierarchy.
- Decorative SaaS styling, glass effects, gradients, or oversized metrics.
- Thick colored side rails, ambiguous status colors, and unexplained evidence labels.
- Copy with em dashes, inflated claims, or completion language unsupported by evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before assertion: every status should connect to proof or clearly state that proof is pending.
2. Decisions should stand out: approval needs and blockers must be easier to find than completed background work.
3. Plain language first: use short client-facing explanations, with technical detail available through links.
4. Consistent states: use one visual and verbal vocabulary for done, in progress, waiting, blocked, and pending verification.
5. Preserve trust: never imply that Google refreshed, a publisher replied, or a source changed without direct evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile layouts, adequate text size, non-color status cues, and readable contrast. Interactive controls must provide at least a 44 by 44 pixel touch target. The tracker is English-first but should use logical CSS properties where practical so future Arabic or right-to-left adaptation remains possible.
