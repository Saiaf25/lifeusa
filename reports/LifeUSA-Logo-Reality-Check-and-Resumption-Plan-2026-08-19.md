## What Google actually allows
There is no general Google complaint that removes a truthful but old logo merely because the organization no longer prefers it.

The supported sequence is:

1. change or remove the old image at the source;

2. request recrawling for changed LifeUSA-owned URLs when appropriate;

3. use Refresh Outdated Content for a third-party page and image only after the publisher has removed or materially replaced the image;

4. use Search Console Removals only for eligible LifeUSA-controlled URLs, recognizing that temporary removal is not a permanent source fix;

5. manage Business Profile photos through an authorized profile manager;

6. suggest a Knowledge Panel image change only if a separate panel exists and its image is wrong; and

7. use a legal or trademark complaint only when a genuine legal basis exists and counsel or an authorized representative approves it.


Google Ads logo assets affect paid results only. They do not repair organic Google Images.

Primary Google references are collected in [the August 19 current-source research](../technical-seo-fixes/google-logo-replacement-status/evidence/2026-08-19/google-logo-current-source-research.md).
