Baseline

LifeUSA has strong program material, but search signals, donor routing, and content planning sit across several surfaces. Month one identified where SEO work can improve pages and where platform limits constrain work.

Audit queue

The first audit logged 54 SEO issues. Five major gaps shaped the first remediation queue: domain and subdomain structure, noindex coverage, language signals, nonprofit schema, and image/page experience.

Search visibility

Google showed LifeUSA pages in search. Low click rates pointed to page titles, descriptions, page roles, search-result clarity, and image appearance.

Donor routing

LifeUSA uses multiple website and donation surfaces. Leadership needs clean reporting across those paths, and some SEO edits depend on another platform.

Content workflow

The team needed a repeatable way to choose topics, turn program knowledge into article plans, and connect articles back to donor decisions.

Work Enablers

Website and domain access

Wix and GoDaddy access let us inspect the website setup, domain setup, and controllable SEO surfaces.

Search Console data

Search Console let us pull current clicks, impressions, click rate, average position, and top landing pages for the report period.

Measurement ownership

Analytics and Tag Manager still need confirmed owners before LifeUSA changes tracking or uses donor-path reporting in future reports.

Donor Portal limit

Salesforce does not allow page-level SEO optimization on Donor Portal pages. SEO work needs to happen around those pages through controllable website pages, internal links, redirects, sitemaps, and campaign paths.
