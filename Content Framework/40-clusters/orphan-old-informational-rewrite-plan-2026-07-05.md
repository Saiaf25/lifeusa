### 1. Rewrite: Who Will Watch Over the Orphans of Gaza?

Existing article:

```text
https://www.lifeusa.org/post/who-will-watch-over-the-orphans-of-gaza
```

Recommended rewrite title:

```text
Gaza Orphans: How War Leaves Children Without Care, Safety, and Support
```

Primary keyword ownership:

- `gaza orphans` - 170/mo

Secondary keyword support:

- `war orphans` - 390/mo
- `orphan relief` - 40/mo
- `orphan crisis` - 10/mo

Why this is worthy:

- It is not a generic orphan definition page.
- It answers a crisis-specific informational question: what happens to orphaned children in Gaza and why their needs are different in conflict.
- LifeUSA has several Gaza orphan support examples that can make the page more useful than a generic appeal.
- It can support the orphan pillar without competing with it.

Rewrite angle:

- Explain how war creates orphanhood, family separation, caregiver loss, displacement, hunger, trauma, and interrupted schooling.
- Use careful, source-backed language and avoid political overreach.
- Show what support can look like: food packs, baby formula and essentials, winter relief, Eid/joy support, and broader relief.

Internal links and proof examples:

1. `Who Will Watch Over the Orphans of Gaza?` -> enrich existing article around `Gaza Orphans: How War Leaves Children Without Care, Safety, and Support`.
