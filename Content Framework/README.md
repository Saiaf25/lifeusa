# LifeUSA Content Framework

This folder is the operating system for LifeUSA content work. It is not a set of finished articles. It defines how content topics are chosen, briefed, drafted, reviewed, handed off, and improved for Life for Relief and Development.

## Scope statement

We are creating a content framework for LifeUSA, a USA-based humanitarian nonprofit. The framework should help the team produce donor-facing and search-informed content that improves organic discovery, supports donation and sponsorship journeys, and gives Angela, Hala, Tasneem, Humza, and Saiaf a shared content process. It follows the Makeen framework structure, but the strategy is rebuilt for nonprofit humanitarian aid, USA donor trust, English-first publishing with Arabic support, image/news/report visibility, AI-readable article structure, and ethical beneficiary storytelling.

## How this client differs from Makeen

Makeen's reference framework is transactional and real-estate oriented. LifeUSA's framework is trust-and-education plus mission-led nonprofit content. The key risks are not property regulation or project inventory freshness. They are accuracy, dignity, donor trust, claims that overpromise impact, outdated campaign pages, duplicated URLs, weak metadata, image visibility, and content that does not connect field reports to donor intent.

## Operating rules

1. Every new content idea starts with inventory and intent checks.
2. Update an existing URL before creating a new one when the intent already exists.
3. Treat donor trust pages as first-class SEO targets, not only brand pages.
4. Do not make unverifiable program, beneficiary, financial, medical, or legal claims.
5. Keep beneficiary dignity intact. No sensational framing.
6. For orphan, widow, Gaza, Ramadan, water, health, and emergency topics, cite LifeUSA source pages or verified reports before drafting.
7. For image visibility requests, brief alt text, captions, filenames, dimensions, and page context together.
8. Keep donation CTAs clear, but do not make every article feel like a donation page.
9. English is the main current surface. Arabic is a parallel audience and must not inherit broken hreflang or copied English assumptions.
10. Zoho Mail is the current live source for `hello@saiaf.me` LifeUSA threads when authenticated. Use the saved comms log as the durable local record, then refresh with Zoho Mail before making new claims from email.
